### Design & Engineering Directives
1. **Visual Quality**: Sleek dark mode, fluid glassmorphism, dynamic glowing accents, and high visual polish.
2. **Animation**: Natural physics-based easing, cursor-following spotlights, and reactive avatar/character visuals.
3. **Accessibility & Standards**: WCAG 2.1 AA compliant, native HTML5 APIs (Popover, Dialog, Anchor Positioning, Container Queries).
4. **Performance**: Zero-jank interactions, `scheduler.yield()` for batch tasks, and optimized rendering.
